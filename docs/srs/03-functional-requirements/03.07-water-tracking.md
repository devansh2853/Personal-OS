# 3.7 Functional Requirements — Water Tracking

---

# 3.7.1 Purpose

The Water Tracking module enables users to record daily water intake, monitor hydration progress, and track consumption against a configurable daily hydration goal.

The module supports quick logging, custom intake amounts, historical records, and future integration with smart hydration devices.

---

# 3.7.2 Scope

This module includes:

- Water Intake Logging
- Daily Hydration Tracking
- Hydration Goals
- Water History

This module does not include:

- Nutrition Tracking
- Electrolyte Tracking
- Smart Bottle Integration
- Health Device Synchronization

---

# 3.7.3 Business Rules

### BR-WA-001

Each water intake record shall belong to exactly one user.

---

### BR-WA-002

Each water intake record shall have a unique identifier.

---

### BR-WA-003

A user may log multiple water intake records per day.

---

### BR-WA-004

Daily hydration totals shall be calculated from all water intake records for the selected day.

---

### BR-WA-005

Water volume shall be stored using a standard internal unit.

---

### BR-WA-006

Users may configure their preferred display unit.

Examples:

- mL
- L
- fl oz (future)

---

# 3.7.4 Functional Requirements

## Water Logging

### FR-WA-001

The system shall allow users to log water intake.

---

### FR-WA-002

Users shall be able to select a predefined intake amount.

Examples:

- 250 mL
- 500 mL
- 750 mL
- 1000 mL

---

### FR-WA-003

Users shall be able to enter a custom water intake amount.

---

### FR-WA-004

Users shall be able to specify the intake date and time.

---

### FR-WA-005

If no timestamp is provided, the system shall use the current date and time.

---

## Water Management

### FR-WA-006

Users shall be able to edit water intake records.

---

### FR-WA-007

Users shall be able to delete water intake records.

---

### FR-WA-008

Deleting a water intake record shall require confirmation.

---

## Daily Hydration

### FR-WA-009

The system shall calculate the user's daily water intake.

---

### FR-WA-010

The system shall display progress toward the daily hydration goal.

---

### FR-WA-011

The system shall recalculate daily totals immediately after adding, editing, or deleting a water intake record.

---

## Hydration Goals

### FR-WA-012

Users shall be able to define a daily hydration goal.

---

### FR-WA-013

Users shall be able to update their hydration goal.

---

### FR-WA-014

Updating the hydration goal shall not modify historical water intake records.

---

## Water History

### FR-WA-015

The system shall display historical water intake records.

---

### FR-WA-016

The system shall display daily hydration totals.

---

### FR-WA-017

Users shall be able to view individual intake events.

---

## Validation

### FR-WA-018

Water intake values shall be greater than zero.

---

### FR-WA-019

The system shall reject invalid water intake values.

---

### FR-WA-020

Water intake timestamps shall not be in the future.

---

## Future Compatibility

### FR-WA-021

The system architecture shall support reusable container presets.

---

### FR-WA-022

The system architecture shall support smart bottle integration.

---

### FR-WA-023

The system architecture shall support Apple Health and Google Fit synchronization.

---

### FR-WA-024

The system architecture shall support hydration reminders based on intake patterns.

---

# 3.7.5 Acceptance Criteria

The Water Tracking module shall be considered complete when:

- Users can log water intake.
- Users can use predefined or custom amounts.
- Users can edit and delete intake records.
- Daily hydration totals are calculated correctly.
- Progress toward the hydration goal is displayed.
- Historical water intake is available.

---

# 3.7.6 Edge Cases

The system shall correctly handle:

- Multiple intake events within a short period.
- Large custom intake values.
- Editing historical intake records.
- Deleting the only intake record for a day.
- Unit preference changes.

---

# 3.7.7 Out of Scope

The following features are outside Phase 1:

- Smart bottle integration.
- Automatic hydration detection.
- Electrolyte tracking.
- Beverage classification.
- Social hydration challenges.

---

# 3.7.8 Future Enhancements

Potential future improvements include:

- Reusable bottle presets.
- Beverage tracking.
- Electrolyte tracking.
- Smart bottle synchronization.
- Weather-based hydration recommendations.
- AI hydration coaching.
- Apple Health integration.
- Google Fit integration.
