# 3.8 Functional Requirements — Supplement Tracking

---

# 3.8.1 Purpose

The Supplement Tracking module enables users to manage their supplement regimen, record supplement intake, and monitor adherence to scheduled supplementation.

The module provides historical supplement records and supports future reminder and analytics features.

---

# 3.8.2 Scope

This module includes:

- Supplement Management
- Supplement Intake Logging
- Daily Supplement Tracking
- Supplement History

This module does not include:

- Medication Tracking
- Reminder Scheduling
- Nutrition Tracking
- Health Analytics

---

# 3.8.3 Business Rules

### BR-SU-001

Each supplement shall belong to exactly one user.

---

### BR-SU-002

Each supplement intake record shall belong to exactly one supplement.

---

### BR-SU-003

Users may manage multiple supplements.

---

### BR-SU-004

Users may log multiple supplement intake records per day.

---

### BR-SU-005

Historical supplement intake records shall remain independent of future supplement edits.

### BR-SU-006

Every SupplementLog shall reference exactly one Supplement.
A SupplementLog may optionally reference the SupplementSchedule from which it was generated.
SupplementLogs store the dosage used at the time of intake as a historical snapshot.

---

# 3.8.4 Functional Requirements

## Supplement Management

### FR-SU-001

The system shall allow users to create supplements.

---

### FR-SU-002

Users shall be able to edit supplement details.

---

### FR-SU-003

Users shall be able to delete supplements.

---

### FR-SU-004

Deleting a supplement shall require confirmation.

---

### FR-SU-005

Deleting a supplement shall not modify historical supplement intake records.

---

### FR-SU-006

Users shall specify:

- Supplement name
- Dosage
- Dosage unit
- Notes (optional)

---

### FR-SU-007

The system shall support dosage units including:

- mg
- g
- mcg
- IU
- mL
- Capsules
- Tablets
- Scoops
- Drops
- Softgels

---

## Intake Logging

### FR-SU-008

Users shall be able to record supplement intake.

---

### FR-SU-009

Users shall specify the intake date and time.

---

### FR-SU-010

If no timestamp is provided, the system shall use the current date and time.

---

### FR-SU-011

Users shall be able to modify the dosage for an individual intake.

---

### FR-SU-012

Users shall be able to record optional intake notes.

---

## Daily Tracking

### FR-SU-013

The system shall display supplements scheduled or logged for the current day.

---

### FR-SU-014

The system shall display whether a supplement has been taken.

---

### FR-SU-015

The system shall calculate daily adherence based on scheduled versus logged intake.

---

## History

### FR-SU-016

The system shall display historical supplement intake records.

---

### FR-SU-017

Users shall be able to filter supplement history by supplement.

---

### FR-SU-018

Users shall be able to edit supplement intake records.

---

### FR-SU-019

Users shall be able to delete supplement intake records.

---

## Validation

### FR-SU-020

Dosage values shall be greater than zero.

---

### FR-SU-021

Supplement intake timestamps shall not be in the future.

---

### FR-SU-022

The system shall reject invalid dosage values.

---

## Future Compatibility

### FR-SU-023

The system architecture shall support recurring supplement schedules.

---

### FR-SU-024

The system architecture shall support reminder notifications.

---

### FR-SU-025

The system architecture shall support supplement inventory tracking.

---

### FR-SU-026

The system architecture shall support barcode scanning.

---

### FR-SU-027

The system architecture shall support medication tracking.

---

# 3.8.5 Acceptance Criteria

The Supplement Tracking module shall be considered complete when:

- Users can create supplements.
- Users can edit supplement details.
- Users can delete supplements.
- Users can log supplement intake.
- Supplement history is maintained.
- Daily adherence is calculated correctly.

---

# 3.8.6 Edge Cases

The system shall correctly handle:

- Multiple doses of the same supplement in one day.
- Variable dosages.
- Deleted supplements with historical intake records.
- Missed doses.
- Supplements without scheduled intake.

---

# 3.8.7 Out of Scope

The following features are outside Phase 1:

- Medication management.
- Drug interaction checking.
- Prescription management.
- Automatic refill tracking.
- Pharmacy integration.
- Barcode scanning.

---
