# 3.5 Functional Requirements — Weight Tracking

---

# 3.5.1 Purpose

The Weight Tracking module enables users to record, monitor, and review body weight over time.

The module provides historical weight records that support progress tracking, health monitoring, analytics, and future AI-powered recommendations.

---

# 3.5.2 Scope

This module includes:

- Weight Logging
- Weight History
- Weight Editing
- Weight Deletion
- Progress Monitoring
- Weight Goals

This module does not include:

- Nutrition Tracking
- Body Measurements
- Body Fat Percentage
- Analytics Dashboards

---

# 3.5.3 Business Rules

### BR-WE-001

Each weight record shall belong to exactly one user.

---

### BR-WE-002

Each weight record shall have a unique identifier.

---

### BR-WE-003

Each weight record shall contain one body weight measurement.

---

### BR-WE-004

Multiple weight entries may exist for the same calendar day.

---

### BR-WE-005

Weight values shall be stored using a standard internal unit regardless of the user's preferred display unit.

---

### BR-WE-006

Users may view weight values using their preferred measurement unit.

---

### BR-WE-007

Historical weight entries remain independent of future unit preference changes.

---

# 3.5.4 Functional Requirements

## Weight Logging

### FR-WE-001

The system shall allow authenticated users to log body weight.

---

### FR-WE-002

Users shall be able to specify the measurement date and time.

---

### FR-WE-003

If no measurement date and time are provided, the system shall use the current date and time.

---

### FR-WE-004

The system shall allow users to record optional notes.

Example:

- Morning fasted
- After workout
- Before dinner

---

### FR-WE-005

The system shall display weight using the user's preferred measurement unit.

---

## Weight History

### FR-WE-006

The system shall display historical weight entries.

---

### FR-WE-007

Weight history shall be ordered from newest to oldest.

---

### FR-WE-008

Each history entry shall display:

- Weight
- Date
- Time
- Notes (if available)

---

### FR-WE-009

The system shall allow users to view complete details of a weight entry.

---

## Weight Management

### FR-WE-010

The system shall allow users to edit existing weight entries.

---

### FR-WE-011

The system shall allow users to delete weight entries.

---

### FR-WE-012

Deleting a weight entry shall require confirmation.

---

## Weight Goals

### FR-WE-013

The system shall allow users to define a target body weight.

---

### FR-WE-014

The system shall display progress toward the target weight.

---

### FR-WE-015

The system shall allow users to update their target weight.

---

### FR-WE-016

Updating a target weight shall not modify historical weight records.

---

## Validation

### FR-WE-017

Weight values shall be greater than zero.

---

### FR-WE-018

The system shall reject invalid weight values.

---

### FR-WE-019

Measurement timestamps shall not be in the future.

---

## Future Compatibility

### FR-WE-020

The system architecture shall support body fat percentage tracking.

---

### FR-WE-021

The system architecture shall support body measurements.

---

### FR-WE-022

The system architecture shall support progress photos.

---

### FR-WE-023

The system architecture shall support smart scale integration.

---

### FR-WE-024

The system architecture shall support automatic weight synchronization from connected devices.

---

# 3.5.5 Acceptance Criteria

The Weight Tracking module shall be considered complete when:

- Users can log body weight.
- Users can edit weight entries.
- Users can delete weight entries.
- Users can review historical weight records.
- Users can define a target weight.
- Weight values respect the user's preferred measurement unit.

---

# 3.5.6 Edge Cases

The system shall correctly handle:

- Multiple weight entries on the same day.
- Unit preference changes.
- Editing historical entries.
- Extremely large or extremely small values.
- Missing notes.
- Duplicate weight values.

---

# 3.5.7 Out of Scope

The following features are outside Phase 1:

- Smart scale synchronization.
- Progress photos.
- Body fat percentage.
- Waist, chest, arm, and leg measurements.
- BMI calculations.
- AI weight predictions.

---

# 3.5.8 Future Enhancements

Potential future improvements include:

- Body measurements.
- Progress photos.
- Smart scale integration.
- Body composition tracking.
- Automatic trend detection.
- AI-powered weight forecasting.
- Goal recommendations.
