# 3.4.4 Functional Requirements — Workout History & Personal Records

---

# 3.4.4.1 Purpose

The Workout History & Personal Records module enables users to review previously completed workout sessions, monitor exercise progression, and view personal records achieved during training.

This module provides historical access to workout data while preserving the integrity of completed workout sessions.

---

# 3.4.4.2 Scope

This module includes:

- Workout History
- Workout Details
- Exercise History
- Personal Records
- Workout Search
- Workout Deletion

This module does not include:

- Workout Templates
- Workout Sessions
- Set Logging
- Analytics Dashboards
- AI Recommendations

---

# 3.4.4.3 Business Rules

### BR-WT-H-001

Only completed workout sessions shall appear in workout history.

---

### BR-WT-H-002

Historical workout sessions shall remain immutable.

---

### BR-WT-H-003

Deleting a workout session permanently removes it from history.

---

### BR-WT-H-004

Deleting a workout session may affect calculated personal records and analytics.

---

### BR-WT-H-005

Personal records shall always be calculated from existing completed workout sessions.

---

# 3.4.4.4 Functional Requirements

## Workout History

### FR-WT-H-001

The system shall display a chronological list of completed workout sessions.

---

### FR-WT-H-002

Each workout history entry shall display:

- Workout name
- Workout date
- Workout duration
- Number of exercises
- Number of completed sets

---

### FR-WT-H-003

The system shall allow users to open a workout history entry.

---

### FR-WT-H-004

The system shall preserve the workout exactly as it was performed.

---

## Workout Details

### FR-WT-H-005

The system shall display all exercises completed during the workout.

---

### FR-WT-H-006

The system shall display all logged sets.

---

### FR-WT-H-007

The system shall display exercise notes recorded during the workout.

---

### FR-WT-H-008

The system shall display workout notes.

---

### FR-WT-H-009

The system shall display workout duration.

---

## Exercise History

### FR-WT-H-010

The system shall allow users to view the workout history of an individual exercise.

---

### FR-WT-H-011

Exercise history shall display each completed performance of the selected exercise.

---

### FR-WT-H-012

Exercise history shall include:

- Date
- Weight
- Repetitions
- Sets
- Workout name

---

### FR-WT-H-013

Exercise history shall be ordered from newest to oldest.

---

## Personal Records

### FR-WT-H-014

The system shall calculate personal records for each exercise.

---

### FR-WT-H-015

The system shall identify the heaviest weight lifted for an exercise.

---

### FR-WT-H-016

The system shall identify the highest number of repetitions completed for an exercise at a given weight.

---

### FR-WT-H-017

The system shall identify the highest training volume completed for an exercise during a workout.

---

### FR-WT-H-018

The system shall identify new personal records achieved during a workout.

---

### FR-WT-H-019

The system shall visually highlight newly achieved personal records.

---

## Search & Filtering

### FR-WT-H-020

The system shall allow users to search workout history.

---

### FR-WT-H-021

The system shall allow users to filter workout history by date.

---

### FR-WT-H-022

The system shall allow users to filter workout history by workout template.

---

### FR-WT-H-023

The system shall allow users to filter workout history by exercise.

---

## Workout Management

### FR-WT-H-024

The system shall allow users to delete completed workout sessions.

---

### FR-WT-H-025

Deleting a workout session shall require confirmation.

---

### FR-WT-H-026

Deleting a workout session shall automatically update personal records if necessary.

---

### FR-WT-H-027

Deleting a workout session shall automatically update workout analytics if necessary.

---

## Future Compatibility

### FR-WT-H-028

The system architecture shall support workout duplication.

---

### FR-WT-H-029

The system architecture shall support exporting workout history.

---

### FR-WT-H-030

The system architecture shall support workout photos.

---

### FR-WT-H-031

The system architecture shall support workout location tracking.

---

### FR-WT-H-032

The system architecture shall support workout attachments.

---

# 3.4.4.5 Acceptance Criteria

The Workout History & Personal Records module shall be considered complete when:

- Users can browse completed workouts.
- Users can view workout details.
- Users can view exercise history.
- Personal records are calculated correctly.
- New personal records are identified.
- Users can search workout history.
- Users can filter workout history.
- Users can delete completed workouts.

---

# 3.4.4.6 Edge Cases

The system shall correctly handle:

- Empty workout history.
- Deleted workout templates.
- Deleted exercises from future library updates.
- Workouts containing skipped exercises.
- Multiple personal records achieved during one workout.
- Deleting the workout that contains a current personal record.

---

# 3.4.4.7 Out of Scope

The following features are outside Phase 1:

- Social workout sharing.
- Workout likes and comments.
- Public workout history.
- Team leaderboards.
- Global personal records.
- Coach review of workout history.
