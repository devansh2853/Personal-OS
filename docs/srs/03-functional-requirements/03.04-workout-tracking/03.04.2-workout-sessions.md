# 3.4.2 Functional Requirements — Workout Sessions

---

# 3.4.2.1 Purpose

The Workout Sessions module manages the execution of workout templates and ad-hoc workouts.

A workout session represents a single workout performed by a user at a specific point in time. It captures all workout activity, including exercises performed, sets completed, duration, notes, and completion status.

Workout sessions are immutable historical records once completed, except where explicitly permitted by the application.

---

# 3.4.2.2 Scope

This module includes:

- Starting Workout Sessions
- Workout Session Lifecycle
- Exercise Sessions
- Session Editing
- Session Notes
- Session Status
- Session Completion
- Template Updates

This module does not include:

- Workout Templates
- Set Logging
- Workout History
- Personal Records

---

# 3.4.2.3 Business Rules

### BR-WT-S-001

Each workout session shall belong to exactly one user.

---

### BR-WT-S-002

Each workout session shall have a unique identifier.

---

### BR-WT-S-003

A workout session may originate from:

- A workout template
- An empty workout

---

### BR-WT-S-004

A workout session is independent of its originating workout template after creation.

---

### BR-WT-S-005

Changes made during a workout session shall not modify the originating template unless the user explicitly chooses to update the template.

---

### BR-WT-S-006

A user may have only one active workout session at a time.

---

### BR-WT-S-007

Completed workout sessions become historical records.

---

### BR-WT-S-008

Historical workout sessions shall preserve the workout exactly as it was performed, even if the originating template changes later.

---

# 3.4.2.4 Functional Requirements

## Starting Workouts

### FR-WT-S-001

The system shall allow users to start a workout session from a workout template.

---

### FR-WT-S-002

The system shall allow users to start an empty workout session.

---

### FR-WT-S-003

The system shall copy all exercises from the selected template into the new workout session.

---

### FR-WT-S-004

The system shall copy exercise configurations from the template.

---

### FR-WT-S-005

The system shall record the workout start timestamp.

---

### FR-WT-S-006

The system shall initialize the workout session with an Active status.

---

## Active Workout

### FR-WT-S-007

The system shall display exercises in their configured order.

---

### FR-WT-S-008

The system shall allow users to add exercises during an active workout.

---

### FR-WT-S-009

The system shall allow users to remove exercises during an active workout.

---

### FR-WT-S-010

Removing an exercise shall only affect the current workout session.

---

### FR-WT-S-011

The system shall allow users to reorder exercises during an active workout.

---

### FR-WT-S-012

Exercise order changes shall only affect the active workout session.

---

### FR-WT-S-013

The system shall allow users to skip exercises.

---

### FR-WT-S-014

Skipped exercises shall remain part of the workout record.

---

### FR-WT-S-015

The system shall allow users to return to skipped exercises before completing the workout.

---

### FR-WT-S-016

The system shall display exercise notes copied from the workout template.

---

## Session Notes

### FR-WT-S-017

The system shall allow users to add notes to the workout session.

---

### FR-WT-S-018

Workout session notes shall not modify template notes.

---

## Session Status

### FR-WT-S-019

The system shall support the following workout statuses:

- Active
- Paused
- Completed
- Cancelled

---

### FR-WT-S-020

The system shall allow users to pause an active workout.

---

### FR-WT-S-021

The system shall allow users to resume a paused workout.

---

### FR-WT-S-022

The system shall allow users to cancel an active workout.

---

### FR-WT-S-023

Cancelling a workout shall require user confirmation.

---

### FR-WT-S-024

Cancelled workouts shall not be included in workout analytics unless explicitly configured.

---

## Session Completion

### FR-WT-S-025

The system shall allow users to complete a workout session.

---

### FR-WT-S-026

The system shall record the workout completion timestamp.

---

### FR-WT-S-027

The system shall calculate the workout duration.

---

### FR-WT-S-028

The system shall preserve all exercises and logged sets upon completion.

---

### FR-WT-S-029

Completed workout sessions shall become read-only historical records.

---

## Template Updates

### FR-WT-S-030

If a workout session differs from its originating template, the system shall allow the user to update the template after completing the workout.

---

### FR-WT-S-031

Updating a template shall require explicit user confirmation.

---

### FR-WT-S-032

Updating a template shall modify only the workout template.

---

### FR-WT-S-033

Historical workout sessions shall never be modified by template updates.

---

## Recovery

### FR-WT-S-034

The system shall automatically save workout progress during an active workout.

---

### FR-WT-S-035

The system shall restore an interrupted workout session after an unexpected application restart.

---

### FR-WT-S-036

The system shall prevent duplicate active workout sessions.

---

# 3.4.2.5 Acceptance Criteria

The Workout Sessions module shall be considered complete when:

- Users can start workouts from templates.
- Users can start empty workouts.
- Users can add or remove exercises.
- Users can reorder exercises.
- Users can pause and resume workouts.
- Users can cancel workouts.
- Users can complete workouts.
- Workout duration is recorded.
- Session changes remain independent from templates.
- Users may optionally update the template after completing a modified workout.

---

# 3.4.2.6 Edge Cases

The system shall correctly handle:

- Application crash during an active workout.
- Device restart during a workout.
- Network loss during a workout.
- Starting a second workout while another is active.
- Completing a workout with skipped exercises.
- Completing a workout without logging any sets.
- Cancelling immediately after starting.
- Deleting the originating template while a workout session is active.

---

# 3.4.2.7 Out of Scope

The following features are outside Phase 1:

- Live workout sharing.
- Multi-user workouts.
- Coach-monitored workouts.
- Live timers synchronized across devices.
- Workout streaming.
- AI-assisted live coaching.

---

- Live coaching.
