# 3.6.2 Functional Requirements — Nutrition Tracking

---

# 3.6.2.1 Purpose

The Nutrition Tracking module enables users to log meals and food consumption throughout the day while monitoring nutritional intake against personal goals.

---

# 3.6.2.2 Scope

This module includes:

- Food Logging
- Meal Logging
- Daily Nutrition
- Nutrition Goals
- Meal History

This module does not include:

- Food Database
- Saved Meals
- Favorite Foods

---

# 3.6.2.3 Business Rules

### BR-NU-001

Every meal log belongs to exactly one user.

---

### BR-NU-002

A meal log may contain one or more food items.

---

### BR-NU-003

Daily nutritional totals shall be calculated from logged food items.

---

### BR-NU-004

Nutrition goals are user-specific.

---

# 3.6.2.4 Functional Requirements

## Food Logging

### FR-NU-001

Users shall be able to log individual foods.

---

### FR-NU-002

Users shall be able to log saved meals.

---

### FR-NU-003

Users shall be able to log multiple foods within a meal.

---

### FR-NU-004

Users shall be able to modify serving quantities before logging.

---

### FR-NU-005

The system shall calculate nutritional values based on serving quantities.

---

## Meal Logging

### FR-NU-006

Users shall assign logged foods to a meal type.

---

### FR-NU-007

Supported meal types shall include:

- Breakfast
- Morning Snack
- Lunch
- Afternoon Snack
- Dinner
- Supper
- Other

---

### FR-NU-008

Users shall specify the meal date and time.

---

### FR-NU-009

The system shall default the meal timestamp to the current date and time.

---

## Daily Nutrition

### FR-NU-010

The system shall calculate daily calories.

---

### FR-NU-011

The system shall calculate daily protein intake.

---

### FR-NU-012

The system shall calculate daily carbohydrate intake.

---

### FR-NU-013

The system shall calculate daily fat intake.

---

### FR-NU-014

The system shall calculate additional nutrients when available.

Examples:

- Fiber
- Sugar
- Sodium

---

## Nutrition Goals

### FR-NU-015

Users shall define daily calorie goals.

---

### FR-NU-016

Users shall define daily protein goals.

---

### FR-NU-017

Users shall define daily carbohydrate goals.

---

### FR-NU-018

Users shall define daily fat goals.

---

### FR-NU-019

The system shall display progress toward each nutrition goal.

---

## Meal History

### FR-NU-020

Users shall view previously logged meals.

---

### FR-NU-021

Users shall edit meal logs.

---

### FR-NU-022

Users shall delete meal logs.

---

### FR-NU-023

Deleting a meal log shall immediately recalculate daily nutritional totals.

---

## Validation

### FR-NU-024

Serving quantities shall be greater than zero.

---

### FR-NU-025

The system shall reject invalid nutritional values.

---

### FR-NU-026

Meal timestamps shall not be in the future.

---

## Future Compatibility

### FR-NU-027

The system architecture shall support fasting schedules.

---

### FR-NU-028

The system architecture shall support intermittent fasting windows.

---

### FR-NU-029

The system architecture shall support micronutrient tracking.

---

### FR-NU-030

The system architecture shall support AI-powered meal recommendations.

---

# 3.6.2.5 Acceptance Criteria

- Users can log foods.
- Users can log saved meals.
- Daily nutrition totals are calculated correctly.
- Nutrition goals can be configured.
- Meal history is maintained.
- Editing or deleting meals recalculates totals immediately.

---

# 3.6.2.6 Edge Cases

- Logging meals across midnight.
- Multiple meals of the same type.
- Logging custom foods.
- Deleted custom foods referenced by historical meal logs.
- Zero-calorie foods.
- Extremely large serving sizes.

---

# 3.6.2.7 Out of Scope

- AI meal planning.
- Automatic food recognition.
- Continuous glucose monitor integration.
- Dietitian collaboration.
- Social meal sharing.

---

# 3.6.2.8 Future Enhancements

- Weekly meal planning.
- Grocery shopping integration.
- Recipe nutrition analysis.
- AI nutrition coaching.
- Macro cycling.
- Calorie periodization.
