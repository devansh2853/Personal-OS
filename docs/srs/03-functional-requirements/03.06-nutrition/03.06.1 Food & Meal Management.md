# 3.6.1 Functional Requirements — Food & Meal Management

---

# 3.6.1.1 Purpose

The Food & Meal Management module provides a centralized food catalog and reusable meal management system.

It enables users to search foods, create custom foods, manage favorite foods, view recently used foods, and create reusable saved meals for faster nutrition logging.

---

# 3.6.1.2 Scope

This module includes:

- Food Database
- Food Search
- Custom Foods
- Favorite Foods
- Recent Foods
- Saved Meals

This module does not include:

- Food Logging
- Nutrition Goals
- Daily Nutrition
- Meal History

---

# 3.6.1.3 Business Rules

### BR-NU-FM-001

Each food shall have a unique identifier.

---

### BR-NU-FM-002

System foods are read-only.

---

### BR-NU-FM-003

Users may create custom foods.

---

### BR-NU-FM-004

Custom foods belong only to the user who created them.

---

### BR-NU-FM-005

Saved meals belong only to the user who created them.

---

### BR-NU-FM-006

Saved meals contain one or more food items.

---

# 3.6.1.4 Functional Requirements

## Food Database

### FR-NU-FM-001

The system shall maintain a searchable food database.

---

### FR-NU-FM-002

The system shall import food data from an external food database.

---

### FR-NU-FM-003

Imported foods shall be stored in the application's database.

---

### FR-NU-FM-004

The system shall distinguish between system foods and user-created foods.

---

## Food Search

### FR-NU-FM-005

Users shall be able to search foods by name.

---

### FR-NU-FM-006

Search results shall update as the user types.

---

### FR-NU-FM-007

The system shall support partial name matching.

---

### FR-NU-FM-008

The system shall prioritize exact matches.

---

### FR-NU-FM-009

The system shall display serving information.

---

### FR-NU-FM-010

The system shall display nutritional values.

---

## Custom Foods

### FR-NU-FM-011

Users shall be able to create custom foods.

---

### FR-NU-FM-012

Users shall be able to edit their custom foods.

---

### FR-NU-FM-013

Users shall be able to delete their custom foods.

---

## Favorite Foods

### FR-NU-FM-014

Users shall be able to mark foods as favorites.

---

### FR-NU-FM-015

Users shall be able to remove foods from favorites.

---

### FR-NU-FM-016

The system shall display favorite foods separately from search results.

---

## Recent Foods

### FR-NU-FM-017

The system shall maintain a list of recently logged foods.

---

### FR-NU-FM-018

The system shall display recent foods during food search.

---

## Saved Meals

### FR-NU-FM-019

Users shall be able to create saved meals.

---

### FR-NU-FM-020

A saved meal shall contain one or more foods.

---

### FR-NU-FM-021

Users shall be able to edit saved meals.

---

### FR-NU-FM-022

Users shall be able to duplicate saved meals.

---

### FR-NU-FM-023

Users shall be able to delete saved meals.

---

### FR-NU-FM-024

Saved meals shall preserve food quantities.

---

## Validation

### FR-NU-FM-025

The system shall prevent duplicate foods within the imported system database.

---

### FR-NU-FM-026

The system shall validate all nutritional values.

---

### FR-NU-FM-027

The system architecture shall support barcode-based food lookup.

---

### FR-NU-FM-028

The system architecture shall support food images.

---

### FR-NU-FM-029

The system architecture shall support recipe management.

---

# 3.6.1.5 Acceptance Criteria

- Users can search foods.
- Users can create custom foods.
- Users can manage favorite foods.
- Users can access recent foods.
- Users can create saved meals.
- Saved meals preserve food quantities.

---

# 3.6.1.6 Edge Cases

- Duplicate custom foods.
- Foods with missing nutrition data.
- Deleted custom foods used in saved meals.
- Empty saved meals.
- Imported foods with incomplete metadata.

---

# 3.6.1.7 Out of Scope

- Recipe scaling.
- Meal sharing.
- Community food database.
- AI food recognition.
- Barcode scanning.

---
