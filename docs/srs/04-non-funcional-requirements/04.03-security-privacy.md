# 4.3 Non-Functional Requirements — Security & Privacy

---

## Authentication

### NFR-SEC-001

Passwords shall be hashed using a modern adaptive hashing algorithm.

---

### NFR-SEC-002

Access tokens shall have limited lifetimes.

---

### NFR-SEC-003

Refresh tokens shall be securely managed.

---

## Authorization

### NFR-SEC-004

Users shall access only their own resources unless explicitly authorized.

---

### NFR-SEC-005

Every protected endpoint shall validate authentication and authorization.

---

## Data Protection

### NFR-SEC-006

Sensitive data shall be encrypted during transmission using HTTPS.

---

### NFR-SEC-007

Sensitive configuration values shall never be stored in source control.

---

### NFR-SEC-008

Input shall be validated before processing.

---

### NFR-SEC-009

Output shall avoid exposing internal implementation details.

---

## Privacy

### NFR-SEC-010

Users shall be able to delete their own data where supported.

---

### NFR-SEC-011

Personally identifiable information shall be stored securely.

---

### NFR-SEC-012

The system shall minimize collection of unnecessary personal information.

---

## Audit

### NFR-SEC-013

Security-related events shall be logged.

---

### NFR-SEC-014

Authentication failures shall be recorded.

---

### NFR-SEC-015

Administrative actions shall be auditable.

---

## OWASP

### NFR-SEC-016

The application shall mitigate common OWASP Top 10 security risks.

---

### NFR-SEC-017

Rate limiting shall be applied to authentication endpoints.
