# 4.1 Non-Functional Requirements — System Quality Attributes

---

## 4.1.1 Purpose

This section defines the quality attributes that apply to the Personal OS platform.

These requirements describe how the system should behave rather than what functionality it provides.

---

## 4.1.2 Availability

### NFR-QA-001

The system shall be available 99.5% of the time excluding scheduled maintenance.

---

### NFR-QA-002

Scheduled maintenance shall be communicated before deployment.

---

## 4.1.3 Scalability

### NFR-QA-003

The architecture shall support horizontal scaling.

---

### NFR-QA-004

System components shall remain loosely coupled.

---

## 4.1.4 Extensibility

### NFR-QA-005

New modules shall be added without modifying existing business modules whenever possible.

---

### NFR-QA-006

Core business logic shall remain independent of AI features.

---

## 4.1.5 Portability

### NFR-QA-007

The backend shall support mobile and web clients through a common REST API.

---

### NFR-QA-008

The application shall remain cloud-provider independent.

---

## 4.1.6 Compatibility

### NFR-QA-009

The system shall support the latest stable Android and iOS versions targeted by the application.

---

### NFR-QA-010

The backend shall expose versioned APIs.

---

## 4.1.7 Compliance

### NFR-QA-011

The system shall comply with applicable privacy regulations where deployed.

---

### NFR-QA-012

Sensitive health-related data shall be handled securely.
