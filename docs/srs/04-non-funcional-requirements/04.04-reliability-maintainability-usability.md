# 4.4 Non-Functional Requirements — Reliability, Maintainability & Usability

---

## Reliability

### NFR-REL-001

The system shall gracefully handle unexpected failures.

---

### NFR-REL-002

Unexpected errors shall not corrupt stored data.

---

### NFR-REL-003

Automatic backups shall be supported.

---

### NFR-REL-004

Background jobs shall support retries.

---

## Maintainability

### NFR-MNT-001

The project shall use TypeScript strict mode.

---

### NFR-MNT-002

The backend shall follow a feature-based architecture.

---

### NFR-MNT-003

Business logic shall reside in the service layer.

---

### NFR-MNT-004

Input validation shall be centralized.

---

### NFR-MNT-005

Meaningful logging shall be implemented.

---

### NFR-MNT-006

Code shall follow consistent naming conventions.

---

### NFR-MNT-007

Public APIs shall be documented.

---

### NFR-MNT-008

Environment-specific configuration shall not be hardcoded.

---

### NFR-MNT-009

Automated testing shall be supported.

---

## Usability

### NFR-UX-001

The application shall follow a mobile-first design approach.

---

### NFR-UX-002

Common actions shall require minimal user interaction.

---

### NFR-UX-003

Loading indicators shall be displayed during asynchronous operations.

---

### NFR-UX-004

User-friendly error messages shall be presented.

---

### NFR-UX-005

Empty states shall provide helpful guidance.

---

### NFR-UX-006

Interactive touch targets shall meet recommended accessibility sizes.

---

### NFR-UX-007

The interface shall support future localization.

---

### NFR-UX-008

Measurement units shall respect user preferences.
