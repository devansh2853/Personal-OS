# 4.2 Non-Functional Requirements — Performance & Scalability

---

## Performance

### NFR-PERF-001

95% of API requests shall complete within 300 ms under normal operating conditions.

---

### NFR-PERF-002

99% of API requests shall complete within 1 second.

---

### NFR-PERF-003

Long-running operations shall execute as background jobs.

---

### NFR-PERF-004

Frequently accessed data shall support caching.

---

### NFR-PERF-005

API responses shall support pagination where appropriate.

---

## Scalability

### NFR-PERF-006

The backend shall support horizontal scaling.

---

### NFR-PERF-007

Stateless services shall be preferred.

---

### NFR-PERF-008

Session state shall not depend on a single application instance.

---

### NFR-PERF-009

Database indexes shall exist for frequently queried fields.

---

### NFR-PERF-010

Background processing shall use queue-based architecture.

---

### NFR-PERF-011

Large analytical queries shall not block transactional operations.

---

## Capacity

### NFR-PERF-012

The architecture shall support future multi-tenant SaaS deployment.

---

### NFR-PERF-013

Storage growth shall not require architectural redesign.

---

### NFR-PERF-014

File storage shall be independent of application servers.
